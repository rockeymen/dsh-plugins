Project overview

Mobius rewrites itself from your input. Send a change request, a screenshot, or a reference link — Mobius turns them into real code, UI, plugins, or workflow updates, without interrupting your work. Each iteration replaces a plank on the Ship of Theseus, quietly in the background.

View self-evolution examples Serve Every Scenario and Project Serve Every Scenario (toggle the most convienient interface, for all possible scenarios) Manage All Projects (project overview, where you can overwatch all agents working for you) Auto Research

Mobius orchestrates multiple agents into an autonomous research pipeline — reading papers, extracting methods, running experiments, and surfacing results. A research goal becomes a multi-agent system, not a single Q&A. XiaoMo
